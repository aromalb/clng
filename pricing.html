What affects the cost of a website, AI agent or marketing plan?

There is no one-size-fits-all price for digital work, and a generic price list rarely reflects what a business actually needs. Instead, we look at a handful of things that genuinely change the amount of work involved, then give you a clear quote for your project.

  • Scope and features

    The number of pages, the functionality you need (booking, forms, online sales, dashboards) and how custom the design should be.

  • Integrations

    Connecting your website or AI agent to the tools you already use, such as your CRM, email, calendar, accounting or phone system.

  • Content and SEO

    Copywriting, service pages, local landing pages and structured data so your business can be found by the right customers on Google.

  • Ongoing support

    Monitoring, updates, reporting and improvements after launch, which we agree with you as a monthly plan where it makes sense.

Pricing FAQs

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, the features you need (for example bookings, forms or integrations), and how much content and SEO work is involved. Tell us what you need and we will send a clear quote for your specific scope.

Do you offer monthly plans?

Yes. AI agents and content creation and influencer marketing are delivered as monthly plans, quoted to the size and complexity of what you need. Websites and custom software are quoted per project.

Are there hidden costs?

No. Your proposal sets out what is included, so you know what you are getting before you commit. If your requirements change, we talk it through and update the quote with you first.

Is SEO included in your web design?

Yes. Every website we build is SEO-first, with clean structure, fast loading, mobile-friendly layouts, optimised page titles and descriptions, and structured data so search engines can understand your business.

How quickly will I get a quote?

We reply to most enquiries within one working day. Send a short message through the form on this page or email info@nextgreentech.co.uk.

Do you work with businesses outside Cambridgeshire?

Yes. We are based in Huntingdon and work with businesses in Cambridgeshire, London and across the UK, in person or remotely.

Can I combine services in one plan?

Yes. A website with an AI agent behind it, or marketing alongside custom software, can be scoped together as a single plan rather than priced piece by piece.
