Who we are

NextGreenTech UK is a Huntingdon-based digital and software agency. We help businesses build, market and grow their online presence, and we build the systems they run on behind the scenes — websites, apps, billing tools and AI agents.

Rather than piecing together separate vendors for design, advertising, content, engineering and reporting, you work with a single accountable team that manages the full journey — from the first line of website code to the performance data that proves what's working.

As a startup ourselves, we know what early-stage businesses actually need: a website that ranks, a clear plan to reach new clients, and systems that don't fall over as you grow. Every service below can be engaged on its own or combined into a full growth programme.
